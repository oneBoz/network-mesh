3.6.4Demonstrated capabilitiesShown live during development; reproducible with the scripts in the repository.

Self-healing across the internet

15 nodes on 3 devices, Singapore and Japan, through NAT. A killed node is suspected in seconds, convicted, and rediscovered within a probe cycle when revived.

suspect ≈ 2–4 s · rediscovered ≈ 2 s
Encrypted end to end

Every gossip and application frame is AES-256-GCM under a key derived from a shared secret. The preflight refuses to run unencrypted.

AES-256-GCM · HKDF
One-entity decisions

A threat from any node yields the identical assignment and fallback chain on every node. Kill the primary and the next report resolves to the fallback; revive it and the answer returns.

5/5 agree in every check
Replicated engagement lifecycle

A swarm reported from the Mac was matchmade to the Azure VM in about a second, engaged and neutralised from the VM's console, and every device showed the same state.

report → responsible ≈ 1 s
Simulated tracks at 1 Hz

Launched targets stream their trajectory to every device once a second. Four scripted scenarios exercise the chain: cruise missile, two swarm waves, saturation, aircraft probe.

1 update / s · every device
Operational readiness

A judging-day preflight checks dashboard, fleet, encryption, convergence, remote devices, map and cross-node agreement, and answers GO or NO-GO with the reason.

≈ 285 MB per device for the full fleet
